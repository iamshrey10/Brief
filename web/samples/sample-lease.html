RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Every name, address and amount in it is made up, for trying Brief.

1. Parties. This Lease is made between Northwind Property Management LLC ("Landlord") and Alex Rivera ("Tenant").

2. Premises. Landlord leases to Tenant Apartment 4B at Maple Court Apartments, 250 Elm Street, Springfield ("Premises").

3. Term. The Lease begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027.

4. Rent. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,650.00, due on the first day of each month, by bank transfer to the account named by Landlord.

5. Late Fee. If rent is not received by the fifth day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late fee of $75.00, plus $10.00 for each further day the rent remains unpaid, up to a total late fee of $175.00 for that month.

6. Security Deposit. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,650.00 at signing. Landlord shall return the deposit within 21 days after Tenant moves out, less any lawful deductions for unpaid rent or damage beyond normal wear and tear, with a written list of any deductions.

7. Utilities. Tenant pays for electricity and internet. Landlord pays for water, sewer and trash collection.

8. Pets. No pets are allowed on the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. If consent is given, Tenant shall pay a non-refundable pet fee of $300.00.

9. Maintenance and Repairs. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and report any needed repair to Landlord in writing. Landlord shall make necessary repairs within a reasonable time after notice.

10. Entry by Landlord. Landlord may enter the Premises to inspect or make repairs after giving Tenant at least 24 hours' notice, except in an emergency.

11. Early Termination. Tenant may end this Lease before the end of the Term by giving at least 60 days' written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to one month's rent.

12. Subletting. Tenant shall not sublet the Premises or assign this Lease without Landlord's prior written consent.

13. Notices. Notices under this Lease must be in writing and delivered by hand or by email to the addresses the parties give each other.

14. Governing Law. This Lease is governed by the laws of the state in which the Premises are located.
