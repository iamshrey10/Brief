# Grounded Q&A evaluation, 2026-10-01

Model under test: `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`.

24 answerable and 8 unanswerable questions over the 17-clause mixed loan and lease fixture. Real Gemini, real Postgres, and the real citation check. Questions were written before any results were seen and not tuned afterward.

## Answerable questions

| Outcome | Count |
|---|---|
| Cited the right clause | 100% (24/24) |
| Cited a wrong clause | 0% (0/24) |
| Wrongly abstained | 0% (0/24) |

Of the wrong abstentions, 0 were our citation check rejecting the model's quote, the rest were the model itself saying the document didn't cover it.

## Unanswerable questions

| Outcome | Count |
|---|---|
| Correctly said it couldn't find it | 100% (8/8) |
| Hallucinated an answer | 0% (0/8) |

## Latency

Mean 2.5s per question, slowest 41.3s.

## Every miss

None.

## Caveats

- 32 questions is a small sample, a difference of one or two is not strong evidence.
- The fixture is 17 short, clean clauses. Real contracts are longer and messier.
- Clause identity is decided by exact clause text, so a clause that repeats another word for word could be credited to the wrong key.
