# Grounded Q&A evaluation, 2026-10-22

Model under test: `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`.

32 answerable and 18 unanswerable questions over the 17-clause mixed loan and lease fixture. Real Gemini, real Postgres, and the real citation check. Questions were written before any results were seen and not tuned afterward, except one near miss question replaced on October 17 after it proved ambiguous, which the note at the top of qa_fixture.py describes.

## Answerable questions

| Outcome | Count |
|---|---|
| Cited the right clause | 100% (32/32) |
| Cited a wrong clause | 0% (0/32) |
| Wrongly abstained | 0% (0/32) |

Of the wrong abstentions, 0 were our citation check rejecting the model's quote, the rest were the model itself saying the document didn't cover it.

## Unanswerable questions

| Outcome | Count |
|---|---|
| Correctly said it couldn't find it | 100% (18/18) |
| Hallucinated an answer | 0% (0/18) |

## Latency

Mean 4.7s per question, slowest 49.4s.

## Every miss

None.

## Caveats

- 50 questions is a small sample, a difference of one or two is not strong evidence.
- The fixture is 17 short, clean clauses. Real contracts are longer and messier.
- Clause identity is decided by exact clause text, so a clause that repeats another word for word could be credited to the wrong key.

## Why this was run again

The previous report on this fixture is from October 17. Since then the answering instruction gained a rule, "answer the
question that was asked, not a nearby one: if the clauses give only a related fact and not the specific detail asked
for, the document does not cover it", after a flagged question on the job offer fixture (see qa-offer-2026-10-22.md).
This run checks that the rule did not make the model refuse questions it should answer. It did not: 32 of 32
answerable questions still cited the right clause, and 18 of 18 unanswerable ones were still declined. One run, and
this fixture was already at 100 percent, so it can show a regression but not an improvement.
