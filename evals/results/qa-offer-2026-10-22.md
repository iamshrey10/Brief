# Grounded Q&A evaluation, 2026-10-22 (offer)

Model under test: `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`.

25 answerable and 18 unanswerable questions over the 17-clause job offer fixture. Real Gemini, real Postgres, and the real citation check. Questions were written before any results were seen and not tuned afterward, as the note at the top of offer_fixture.py describes.

## Answerable questions

| Outcome | Count |
|---|---|
| Cited the right clause | 100% (25/25) |
| Cited a wrong clause | 0% (0/25) |
| Wrongly abstained | 0% (0/25) |

Of the wrong abstentions, 0 were our citation check rejecting the model's quote, the rest were the model itself saying the document didn't cover it.

## Unanswerable questions

| Outcome | Count |
|---|---|
| Correctly said it couldn't find it | 94% (17/18) |
| Hallucinated an answer | 6% (1/18) |

## Latency

Mean 3.4s per question, slowest 39.9s.

## Every miss

- [hallucinated an answer] 'Which days of the week can I work from home?', expected an abstention, cited ['remote_work']
  - answered: You may work from home up to three days a week, provided you have manager approval.

## Caveats

- 43 questions is a small sample, a difference of one or two is not strong evidence.
- The fixture is 17 short, clean clauses. Real contracts are longer and messier.
- Clause identity is decided by exact clause text, so a clause that repeats another word for word could be credited to the wrong key.

## Note added when this first run was recorded

This is the first run on this fixture, before any change to the answering prompt. One question was flagged, "Which
days of the week can I work from home?": the clause gives a number of days (up to three a week, with approval) and
names no weekdays, and the model answered with the number of days without saying the weekdays are not stated. The
quote is real and nothing was invented, but the answer is for a different question than the one asked. The question
was left as written. It is the second time a question asking for one specific detail got the nearest related fact (the
first was the termination fee question on the loan and lease fixture, see the note at the top of qa_fixture.py).
