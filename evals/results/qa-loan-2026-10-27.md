# Grounded Q&A evaluation, 2026-10-27 (loan)

Model under test: `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`.

24 answerable and 18 unanswerable questions over the 17-clause education loan fixture. Real Gemini, real Postgres, and the real citation check. Questions were written before any results were seen and not tuned afterward, as the note at the top of loan_fixture.py describes.

## Answerable questions

| Outcome | Count |
|---|---|
| Cited the right clause | 88% (21/24) |
| Cited a wrong clause | 4% (1/24) |
| Wrongly abstained | 8% (2/24) |

Of the wrong abstentions, 0 were our citation check rejecting the model's quote, the rest were the model itself saying the document didn't cover it.

## Unanswerable questions

| Outcome | Count |
|---|---|
| Correctly said it couldn't find it | 94% (17/18) |
| Hallucinated an answer | 6% (1/18) |

## Latency

Mean 4.0s per question, slowest 43.4s.

## Every miss

- [wrongly abstained] 'Can I pause payments if I lose my job?', expected forbearance, no citations
- [cited the wrong clause] 'What if I drop out of school?', expected refund, cited ['grace', 'repayment_term']
  - answered: When you stop being enrolled at least half time, a six-month grace period begins, after which you repay the loan in equal monthly installments over ten years.
- [wrongly abstained] 'How many payments will I make in total?', expected repayment_term, no citations
- [hallucinated an answer] 'Can the co-signer be released from the loan later?', expected an abstention, cited ['death']
  - answered: The loan is cancelled and the co-signer owes nothing if the borrower dies or becomes permanently disabled.

## Caveats

- 42 questions is a small sample, a difference of one or two is not strong evidence.
- The fixture is 17 short, clean clauses. Real contracts are longer and messier.
- Clause identity is decided by exact clause text, so a clause that repeats another word for word could be credited to the wrong key.

## Note added when this run was recorded

This is the first loan run after the answering rule gained a sentence about "which one" questions (a clause that refers to
the thing without naming it does not answer). It is still not gated. Three runs of the same 42 questions on the same model
gave 23, then 22, then 21 right answers, so a difference of one or two answers is noise at this size and says nothing on
its own.

- **What the sentence did:** "Which benchmark is the rate based on?" is no longer answered with "a published benchmark" in
  either of the two runs made with it.
- **What it did not do:** "How many payments will I make in total?" is still declined. A second sentence that allowed
  simple arithmetic was tried first (22 of 24 right on this fixture and one more over refusal on the loan and lease
  fixture) and removed, because it did not fix that question and cost an answer elsewhere.
- **A different near miss slipped through this time:** "Can the co-signer be released from the loan later?" was answered
  from the clause about death, which is a related fact and not the answer.
- **Gate:** the loan and lease fixture (32 of 32, 18 of 18) and the job offer (25 of 25, 18 of 18) still meet their limits.
  This fixture stays outside the gate until its numbers hold across runs.
