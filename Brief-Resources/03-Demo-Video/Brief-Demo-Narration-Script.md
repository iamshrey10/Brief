# Brief demo video: narration script

Final video: 89.6 seconds, 1920 by 1080, 30 frames per second. Voice: Kokoro `am_fenrir` at 1.1 times normal speed. 242 spoken words over 89.6 seconds is about 162 words per minute overall, with a pause between sentences. Every sentence is also burned into the picture as a caption and is in the captions file.

The spoken text is written the way it is pronounced when it differs (for example, P D F). The caption text is what appears on screen.
