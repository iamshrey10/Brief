# Brief demo video: storyboard

Total length 89.6 seconds. Captions for every spoken sentence are burned into the picture, and the same sentences are in the captions file.

The dashboard, the Documents page, the key terms, "Before you sign" and both answers are the real Brief website driven through its real API with real Gemini calls on the sample lease that ships with the site (a made-up lease with a made-up tenant, "Alex Rivera"). The sharing and two-step verification screens are the real website screens too, but they came from a stand-in API (see the production notes). Cards and the technology scene use the same fonts and colours as the landing page.
